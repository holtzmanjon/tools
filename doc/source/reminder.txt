reminder module
***************

reminder is a tool that sends email to desired recipients based on dates,
email addresses, and a comment field in a TSV file.  This may be useful
for group based reminders. The TSV file may be downloaded, e.g., from a 
Google doc: this is how it is used for NMSU astroph reminders.

Main driver is executable reminder ::

  reminder [-h] [--ndays NDAYS] [--broadcast BROADCAST] [--header HEADER] [--subject SUBJECT] [--datecol DATECOL] [--emailcol EMAILCOL]
                [--messagecols [MESSAGECOLS ...]] [--messagefmt [MESSAGEFMT ...]] [--addr ADDR] [--individual]

    optional arguments:
    -h, --help            show this help message and exit
    --ndays NDAYS         Look for spreadsheet lines with date < ndays
    --broadcast BROADCAST
                          send email to this broadcast address
    --header HEADER       text of email before spreadsheet lines
    --subject SUBJECT     subject for email
    --datecol DATECOL     spreadsheet column header for column with date
    --emailcol EMAILCOL   spreadsheet column for emails to specific individuals
    --messagecols [MESSAGECOLS ...]
                          spreadsheet columns to include in email
    --messagefmt [MESSAGEFMT ...]
                          format for spreadsheet columns to include in email
    --addr ADDR           Google sheet address
    --individual          send email to the addresses in specified email column
